# Third-party source provenance — control adapters

No external binaries are bundled. Native control remains disabled until signing and hardware gates are met.

Reference: https://github.com/az-code-lab/ampere/tree/843498d7beb711c0e1b18e48c8520debe67f6e3f

Files: `Sources/Shared/NativeChargeLimit.swift`, `Sources/SMCWriter/main.swift`
